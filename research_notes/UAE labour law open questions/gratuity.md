## OQ-006: Does temporary work of one year or more earn gratuity, how is it computed, and does "less than a year" also apply to part-time and job sharing?

### Takeaway
Yes, temporary work of a year or more earns gratuity. The Arabic ER text, MOHRE's own English guidance and the English u.ae page all read ER Art 30(2) as a single exclusion: temporary work shorter than a year. The English ER's "or" wording is not the official reading (confidence: high). No source says how gratuity is computed for temporary work of a year or more (confidence in any method: low to medium). The one-year threshold does reach part-time and job-sharing workers, but through Law Art 51(2)–(3) (the full-time value they are pro-rated from is zero before one year), not through ER Art 30(2). MOHRE, as reported, states a 12-month minimum for part-time (confidence: medium-high).

### Cited Findings
- ER Art 30(2), AR (prevailing): "لا تنطبق مكافأة نهاية الخدمة في حال العمل بنمط التوظيف المؤقت إذا كانت مدته أقل من سنة". This is one conditional exclusion, with no "أو" ("or"). — [ER AR, primary](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/ar/legislations/1547/download)
- ER Art 30(2), EN: "The end of service pay shall not be applicable in the event of operating under a temporary employment model or if its duration is less than a year." This reads as two exclusions. — [ER EN, primary](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1547/download)
- MOHRE's own **English** guidance adopts the Arabic reading: "The end-of-service gratuity does not apply to temporary workers with less than one year service." — [MOHRE "Dear Worker – Know your rights" (EN), Wayback 18 Jan 2026, primary/official guidance](https://web.archive.org/web/20260118075825/https://mohre.gov.ae/en/guidance-and-awareness-portal-new/employee-companies/dear-worker-know-your-rights)
- MOHRE Arabic guidance reproduces the ER text verbatim: "لا تنطبق مكافأة نهاية الخدمة في حال العمل بنمط التوظيف المؤقت إذا كانت مدته أقل من سنة." — [MOHRE "عزيزي العامل" (AR), Wayback 13 Dec 2025, primary](https://web.archive.org/web/20251213042239/https://mohre.gov.ae/ar/guidance-and-awareness-portal-new/employee-companies/dear-worker-know-your-rights)
- u.ae (TDRA government portal), English, "Updated on: 06 Oct 2025": "End-of-service gratuity does not apply in cases of temporary employment, if the duration is less than one year." — [u.ae EN end-of-service page, Wayback 1 May 2026, primary/official guidance](https://web.archive.org/web/20260501200625/https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/end-of-service-benefits-for-employees-in-the-private-sector); the Arabic page has identical ER wording — [u.ae AR, Wayback 11 Feb 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260211011349/https://u.ae/ar-ae/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/end-of-service-benefits-for-employees-in-the-private-sector)
- Law Art 7(1)(c): temporary work is defined by **duration or task** ("work of which nature requires a specific period of time, or which focuses on a specific job and ends by the completion thereof"), not by hours. — [Law EN, primary](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1541/download)
- Law Art 51(2)–(3): a foreign full-time worker earns gratuity only after "one or more years of continuous service", and pro-rata for parts of a year only "provided that he has completed one year of continuous service". — [Law EN, primary](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1541/download)
- Law Art 8(4)–(5): renewals and extensions add to "continuous service". This matters for temporary contracts that are renewed past one year. — [Law EN, primary](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1541/download)
- MOHRE (Twitter guidance as reported by Emarat Al Youm, 16 Sep 2022, citing ER Art 30) on part-time and other patterns: "لا يجوز استحقاق هذه المكافأة في حال لم تتجاوز مدة خدمة العامل 12 شهراً". — [Emarat Al Youm, secondary report of an official statement](https://www.emaratalyoum.com/local-section/careers/2022-09-16-1.1668187). Note the wording "لم تتجاوز" ("did not exceed" 12 months), which differs at exactly 12 months from "أقل من سنة" ("less than a year").
- Secondary press misattributes the one-year rule to part-time: Gulf News says ER Art 30 clause 2 means a part-time worker needs "at least one continuous year of service" — [Gulf News, Zainab Husain, updated 11 Jun 2025, secondary](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/new-uae-labour-law-are-part-time-workers-entitled-to-end-of-service-benefits-1.1652369188838). Khaleej Times quotes the clause as "in the case of part-time employment if its duration is less than one year", a misquote of "temporary". — [Khaleej Times, Ashish Mehta, 28 May 2023, secondary](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/legal/uae-how-will-end-of-service-benefits-gratuity-be-calculated-if-i-take-up-part-time-job)
- Secondary commentary says workers on any non-full-time pattern (part-time, temporary, flexible, remote, job sharing) are eligible after more than one year of service — [Sovereign Group, secondary](https://www.sovereigngroup.com/news/end-of-service-benefits-in-the-uae-a-complete-guide-2024/). Rubert Partners says temporary work under a year earns nothing — [Rubert Partners, secondary](https://www.rubertpartners.com/en/uae-end-of-service-gratuity).

### Inferences
- **Q1 (recommend: yes).** The Arabic prevails. MOHRE's English page and the English u.ae page both paraphrase the rule as "temporary workers with less than one year service", so the official explainers do not treat the English ER "or" as two exclusions. Temporary work of one year or more therefore earns gratuity. Confidence: high.
- **Q2 (recommend, low-medium confidence):** compute it as Art 51 × (contracted annual hours ÷ full-time annual hours). This is not stated by any source. Reasoning: ER Art 30 is the only mechanism made under Law Art 52 for non-full-time patterns, and temporary work is defined by duration, not hours. A temporary worker on full-time hours has a ratio of 100%, so the result equals Law Art 51 exactly (21/30 days per year of basic wage, pro-rata for part years after the first year, two-year cap). A temporary worker on reduced hours gets the same pro-rating as part-time. Both candidate readings ("like full-time under Art 51" and "Art 30(1) pro-rata") therefore give the same result whenever the temporary worker works full-time hours. They differ only for temporary workers on reduced hours, where pro-rating is the more consistent choice.
- **Q3 (recommend: the outcome is the same as the English reading, but on a different legal basis).** Under the prevailing Arabic, ER Art 30(2) itself excludes only temporary work. However, ER Art 30(1) multiplies "the value of the end of service pay under a full-time employment contract", and under Law Art 51(2)–(3) that value is zero until one year of continuous service. So a part-time or job-sharing worker with under a year gets no gratuity in any case. MOHRE's reported "12 months" statement agrees. For the gratuity tool: part-time or job sharing under one year should return no gratuity citing Art 51(2)–(3) together with ER Art 30(1), not ER Art 30(2).
- Exactly 12 months: "أقل من سنة" (Arabic ER) and "one or more years" (Art 51) both give gratuity at exactly one year. The reported MOHRE tweet's "لم تتجاوز 12 شهراً" would deny it. The press wording is weaker than the regulation, so follow the regulation.

### Gaps
- No official source or judgment says how gratuity for temporary work of one year or more is computed. The Q2 recommendation is reasoning, not a sourced rule.
- No court judgment found on ER Art 30(2) or on the English/Arabic discrepancy.
- Could not view the original MOHRE tweet; the "12 months" wording is known only from the Emarat Al Youm report.

## OQ-008: Which basic wage does ER Art 30(1) pro-rate for part-time and job-sharing workers?

### Takeaway
No official source settles this. ER Art 30(1), its Arabic text, MOHRE's EN and AR guidance, u.ae and MOHRE's 2022 statement all repeat "the value of the end-of-service gratuity for the full-time employment contract" without naming a wage. No official calculator or judgment was found. Secondary worked examples use one basic salary without saying whose it is. Recommendation (low-medium confidence, an owner decision): Option B, a full-time-equivalent basic wage. Applying the hours ratio to a gratuity computed on an already-reduced part-time wage discounts the worker twice. Option B gives the same amount as plain Art 51 on the worker's own proportionate wage.

### Cited Findings
- ER Art 30(1), EN: "The annual number of working hours stipulated in the employment contract divided by the annual number of working hours under a full-time contract, multiplied by 100 equals the percentage multiplied by the value of the end of service pay under a full-time employment contract." — [ER EN, primary](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1547/download)
- ER Art 30(1), AR: "...مضروبًا في 100 يساوي النسبة مضروبة في قيمة مكافأة نهاية الخدمة لعقد العمل بالدوام الكامل." It names no wage. — [ER AR, primary](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/ar/legislations/1547/download)
- Law Art 51(5): gratuity "calculated according to the last basic wage received by the foreign Worker" (AR: "وفق آخر أجر أساسي كان يستحقه العامل الأجنبي", i.e. the last basic wage the worker was *entitled to*). — [Law, primary](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/ar/legislations/1541/download)
- ER Art 5(1)(b), job sharing: tasks are divided "وينعكس على قيمة الأجر المستحق لكل منهم بحيث يكون نسبة وتناسب. ويتم التعامل معهم وفق ضوابط العمل الجزئي". The wage is already proportionate, and job sharers are treated under part-time controls. — [ER AR, primary](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/ar/legislations/1547/download)
- MOHRE guidance restates the formula without naming a wage. EN: "...multiplied by the value of the end-of-service gratuity for the full-time employment contract." — [MOHRE EN, Wayback 18 Jan 2026, primary](https://web.archive.org/web/20260118075825/https://mohre.gov.ae/en/guidance-and-awareness-portal-new/employee-companies/dear-worker-know-your-rights); AR identical to the ER — [MOHRE AR, Wayback 13 Dec 2025, primary](https://web.archive.org/web/20251213042239/https://mohre.gov.ae/ar/guidance-and-awareness-portal-new/employee-companies/dear-worker-know-your-rights)
- u.ae: "...the percentage on which the end of service benefit should be calculated. Then this percentage should be multiplied by the value of the end of service benefit due for the full time contract." No wage is named. — [u.ae EN, Wayback 1 May 2026, primary/official guidance](https://web.archive.org/web/20260501200625/https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/end-of-service-benefits-for-employees-in-the-private-sector)
- MOHRE 2022 statement as reported: same formula, no wage named. — [Emarat Al Youm 16 Sep 2022, secondary report](https://www.emaratalyoum.com/local-section/careers/2022-09-16-1.1668187)
- Gulf News example: 1,000 hours against 2,000 full-time hours gives "50% of the full-time gratuity entitlement". The base is described only as "the gratuity amount that would be paid to a full-time employee" (closer to B in wording, but not explicit). — [Gulf News, secondary](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/new-uae-labour-law-are-part-time-workers-entitled-to-end-of-service-benefits-1.1652369188838)
- Khaleej Times (lawyer Q&A) does not say whether the part-time or a full-time-equivalent salary is used. — [Khaleej Times, secondary](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/legal/uae-how-will-end-of-service-benefits-gratuity-be-calculated-if-i-take-up-part-time-job)
- HAS Law Firm (Legal 500, 23 Jan 2026) says only that the law delegates non-full-time calculation to the ER, with no wage specified. — [Legal 500 / HAS Law Firm, secondary](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/united-arab-emirates/employment-and-hr/end-of-service-benefits-in-the-uae-what-employers-and-employees-should-know)

### Inferences
- **The arithmetic decides the stakes.** If the part-time wage is proportionate (W_pt = r × W_ft, where r is the hours ratio), then Option A gives r × Art51(W_pt) = r² × Art51(W_ft). Option B gives r × Art51(W_ft) = Art51(W_pt). In other words, **Option B equals plain Art 51 applied to the worker's own part-time basic wage**, and Option A discounts twice. At 50% hours, A pays a quarter of the full-time figure and B pays half. B matches the press illustrations ("50% of the full-time gratuity entitlement" at 50% hours) if "full-time entitlement" means a full-time employee's gratuity.
- **Arguments for A (own wage):** Art 51(5) names only "the last basic wage" the worker was entitled to, and nothing in Art 30 authorises substituting a notional wage. A needs no hypothetical full-time-equivalent wage, which the contract often doesn't state (and may not exist for a worker with several employers). It is mechanically what a literal reader of "Art 51 value × percentage" does.
- **Arguments for B (full-time-equivalent wage):** the text asks for "the value of the end of service pay **under a full-time employment contract**", and a full-time contract carries a full-time wage. The hours ratio only has a purpose if the base is a full-time amount; otherwise the hours reduction already in the wage is applied again. ER Art 5(1)(b) confirms the wage itself is already proportionate. Law Art 52 requires the mechanism to serve "the interests of both parties", which argues against an r² outcome.
- **Practical recommendation:** adopt B, implemented as "full-time-equivalent basic wage = own basic wage ÷ r" (equivalently, Art 51 on the own wage) when the own wage is proportionate. Keep returning OQ-008 because no authority confirms it. Confidence: low-medium.

### Gaps
- No official MOHRE calculator handling part-time or job sharing was found. The MOHRE site could not be reached directly, and searches returned only unofficial calculators.
- No court judgment found on which wage Art 30(1) uses.
- A search-engine summary attributed to an unidentified blog the claim that part-time gratuity uses "actual basic salary, not a notional full-time salary". I could not trace it (the candidate site, emiratescalculator.com, is a placeholder page), so it is excluded.

## OQ-009: How is a flexible worker's gratuity computed?

### Takeaway
No ER provision, MOHRE guidance or judgment names flexible work in the gratuity rules. The best official indication is u.ae, which describes ER Art 30(1) as applying to "part time contracts **or other types of work arrangements**". The Arabic ER Art 30 heading and opening clause also refer to "other work patterns" and "غير نظام العمل بدوام كامل" ("other than the full-time system"). Recommendation (low-medium confidence): compute flexible workers' gratuity under ER Art 30(1) (contracted or worked annual hours ÷ full-time annual hours × the full-time Art 51 value), with the one-year threshold applying through Art 51. Do not deny it altogether: no source excludes flexible workers.

### Cited Findings
- Law Art 7(1)(d): flexible work is "work of which hours or working days change according to the volume of work and the economic and operational variables of the Employer". — [Law EN, primary](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1541/download)
- Law Art 51 heading: "End-of-Service Gratuity for Full-Time Workers" (AR "للعاملين بنمط الدوام الكامل"). Law Art 52 heading: "...Workers of Other Types of Work". Art 52 delegates to the ER "the mechanism ... for foreign Workers in work types other than full-time". — [Law EN/AR, primary](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1541/download)
- ER Art 30 heading, AR: "مكافأة نهاية الخدمة للعاملين بأنماط العمل الأخرى" (EN: "End of Service Pay for Workers Subject to Other Work Models"). Opening clause, AR: "يكون احتساب مكافأة نهاية الخدمة للعاملين بأنماط الدوام الجزئي أو تقاسم الوظيفة **وبغير نظام العمل بدوام كامل**" (EN: "operating under a part-time or employment sharing model, and not under a full-time model"). — [ER AR](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/ar/legislations/1547/download), [ER EN](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1547/download) (primary)
- ER Art 10(4)(d) lists "Flexible employment contract" (عقد عمل العمل المرن) among the Ministry's approved contract templates. — [ER, primary](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1547/download)
- u.ae, EN section titled "End-of-Service gratuity for part-time and other work models": "the end of service benefits for workers in part time contracts **or other types of work arrangements** are calculated pursuant to the following: [ER Art 30(1) formula]". — [u.ae EN, Wayback 1 May 2026, primary/official guidance](https://web.archive.org/web/20260501200625/https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/end-of-service-benefits-for-employees-in-the-private-sector). The AR page reads "احتساب نهاية الخدمة لعقود العمل بدوام جزئي **وأنماط العمل الأخرى**" — [u.ae AR, Wayback 11 Feb 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260211011349/https://u.ae/ar-ae/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/end-of-service-benefits-for-employees-in-the-private-sector)
- MOHRE's English guidance is narrower: "If you work on a part-time or job-sharing basis, your end-of-service gratuity will be calculated according to the following..." The Arabic copies the ER's "وبغير نظام العمل بدوام كامل". — [MOHRE EN](https://web.archive.org/web/20260118075825/https://mohre.gov.ae/en/guidance-and-awareness-portal-new/employee-companies/dear-worker-know-your-rights), [MOHRE AR](https://web.archive.org/web/20251213042239/https://mohre.gov.ae/ar/guidance-and-awareness-portal-new/employee-companies/dear-worker-know-your-rights) (primary)
- Secondary: Sovereign Group says workers on "any alternative pattern ... including part-time, temporary, flexible, remote working or job sharing" are eligible after more than one year — [Sovereign, secondary](https://www.sovereigngroup.com/news/end-of-service-benefits-in-the-uae-a-complete-guide-2024/). Gulf News advises flexible workers to "check your contract and consult with your HR department" — [Gulf News, secondary](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/new-uae-labour-law-are-part-time-workers-entitled-to-end-of-service-benefits-1.1652369188838). HAS Law Firm says only "part-time, job sharing, etc." are delegated to the ER — [Legal 500, secondary](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/united-arab-emirates/employment-and-hr/end-of-service-benefits-in-the-uae-what-employers-and-employees-should-know)

### Inferences
- **Reading 1, ER Art 30(1) pro-rata (recommended).** For: the ER Art 30 heading is "other work patterns". The Arabic "وبغير نظام العمل بدوام كامل" can be read as an extra category ("and [others] not under the full-time system"), not just a description of part-time and job sharing. The u.ae explainer applies the formula to "other types of work arrangements". Art 30(1) is the only ER mechanism made under Art 52, which covers all non-full-time patterns. Flexible work is by definition variable hours, which is exactly what an hours ratio handles. Against: the opening clause names only part-time and job sharing, and MOHRE's English narrows it to them. Practical issue: a flexible contract may not fix "annual working hours stipulated in the employment contract". Using actual annual hours worked would be an extrapolation with no source.
- **Reading 2, full Art 51.** For: if a flexible worker in fact works full-time hours, the Art 30(1) ratio is 100% and the result equals Art 51 anyway. Against: Art 51 is expressly limited to the "full-time pattern" (نمط الدوام الكامل), and Art 52 sends every other pattern to the ER.
- **Reading 3, no gratuity.** Against: the only exclusion in ER Art 30 is temporary work under a year. Art 52 tells the ER to *regulate* (تنظيم) gratuity for other patterns, not to deny it. No official source says flexible workers get nothing. This reading has no textual support beyond the ER's silence.
- For the gratuity tool: replace `flexible_work_undecided` with the Art 30(1) computation using contracted annual hours, with no gratuity under one year of continuous service (Art 51(2)–(3)). Fall back to "undecided" only when the contract states no hours. Keep citing OQ-009 because the extension is interpretive. Confidence: low-medium.

### Gaps
- No ministerial resolution, MOHRE FAQ or judgment specifically on flexible-work gratuity was found.
- No source says what "annual working hours stipulated in the employment contract" means for a flexible contract whose hours vary.
- I could not retrieve a u.ae "work patterns" page (Wayback returned nothing usable) or a MOHRE page specific to flexible-work permits.
