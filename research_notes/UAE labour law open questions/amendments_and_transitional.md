## Q1. Decree-Laws 14/2022, 20/2023 and 9/2024: dates, Gazette, effective date, articles changed

### Takeaway
There are exactly three amending decree-laws, and each one touches only a few provisions:
- **14/2022** replaced Art 8(3), removing the 3-year cap on fixed-term contracts. It was issued 15 Sep 2022, published in Official Gazette No. 736 on 28 Sep 2022, and in force from 29 Sep 2022 (the Gazette and effective dates are secondary).
- **20/2023** replaced Art 54. It was signed 13 Sep 2023 and in force 1 Jan 2024 under its own Art 2. Its Gazette number was not found.
- **9/2024** replaced Arts 54 and 60 and has a transitional Art 2. It was issued 29 Jul 2024 and published in Official Gazette No. 780 (supplement) on 31 Jul 2024. Its effective date is "30 days after publication" (its Art 4). Sources give 29, 30 or 31 Aug 2024; no source gives 28 Aug. The exact wording of Art 4 was not obtained, so the date stays unresolved. 30 Aug 2024 is the arithmetic reading (see Inferences).

### Inferences
- **Effective date of 9/2024.** It was published 31 Jul 2024 and takes effect 30 days after publication.
  - Counting from the day after publication gives **30 Aug 2024**, which matches Clyde & Co.
  - "31 Aug" is most likely a count that includes both end days, or the first full day.
  - "29 Aug" (MOHRE as reported by Emarat Al Youm) matches counting 30 days from the 29 Jul issue date plus one day.
  - A "28 Aug" date would be 29 Jul + 30 days, i.e. a count from issue rather than publication.
  - Without Art 4's exact words (من تاريخ نشره vs. من تاريخ صدوره), I cannot settle the date. If the corpus needs one date, "30 days after publication on 31 Jul 2024" is the defensible formulation.
- The "1-year limitation from the date the right became due" exists only under 20/2023, from 1 Jan 2024 until 9/2024 took effect. After that the bar is 2 years from the end of the employment relationship.
- **Corrected dates for open-questions.md:** 20/2023 was signed 13 Sep 2023, not 15 Sep (Lexis). 14/2022 is in force from 29 Sep 2022, not from 15 Sep (the issue date).

## Q4. Art 68(2) conversion deadline: extended? What happened to unconverted contracts?

### Takeaway
- **Extended once.** MOHRE Ministerial Resolution No. 27 of 2023 moved the deadline from 1/2 Feb 2023 to **31 Dec 2023** (announced 30 Jan 2023).
- No further extension was found.
- Art 68 itself was never amended.
- No official source says what happens to contracts still unconverted, beyond the text of Art 68:
  - Art 68(1): the Law applies to unlimited contracts concluded under Law 8/1980.
  - Art 68(3): the employer may compute gratuity for them under the 1980 law's unlimited-contract rules.

### Inferences
- The original deadline was one year from 2 Feb 2022, i.e. 1 Feb 2023 (sources say "1 Feb" or "2 Feb").
- The extended deadline of 31 Dec 2023 has passed, and no second ministerial resolution was found.
- The Law does not deem unconverted contracts to be fixed-term. Art 68(1) applies the new Law to them, and Art 68(3) preserves 1980-law gratuity computation "subject to" para (2).
- For the eval, "the deadline was extended to 31 Dec 2023 by MR 27/2023" is supportable, from a secondary source plus a MOHRE headline. Any claim about the legal status of contracts that were not converted should be answered "not stated in the sources".
